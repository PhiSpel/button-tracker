How it works

Button Tracker logs your red and green button presses with a timestamp, stored entirely on this device. No data is ever sent to a server or stored online.

Privacy

  • All data is saved locally in your browser.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no tracking of any kind.
  • Each device is independent — your data never leaves your device.
  • The app works fully offline once loaded.

How to use

  1. Tap a button to log a press (red or green).
  2. Optionally add a comment before pressing.
  3. View your history in the Log tab.
  4. Export as a CSV file from the Export tab.
  5. Install: use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option.
